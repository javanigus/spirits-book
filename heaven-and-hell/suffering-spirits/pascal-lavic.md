Background: Pascal Lavic was a poor seafaring man who died at sea, and whose body was found much later near Le Havre. In August 1863, he came through spontaneously by means of a medium who had never known him and had never heard his name.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Pascal Lavic: I believe in the goodness of God, and I hope He'll take pity on my poor spirit.

Pascal Lavic: I have suffered terribly.

Pascal Lavic: My body perished at sea.

Pascal Lavic: My spirit stayed fastened to my body.

Pascal Lavic: For a long time, it floated on the waves.

Pascal Lavic: God...

Background: The message broke off there. The next day he came back and continued.

Pascal Lavic: God has kindly allowed me to be taken out of the confusion and distress my spirit was plunged into, through the prayers of those I left behind on Earth.

Pascal Lavic: They waited a long time for me.

Pascal Lavic: At last they found my body.

Pascal Lavic: It is now at rest.

Pascal Lavic: And my spirit, which had so much trouble getting free from it, now sees the faults it committed.

Pascal Lavic: When the trial is over, God judges justly, and His goodness reaches those who repent.

Pascal Lavic: If my spirit stayed tossed around with my body for so long, it was because I had to atone.

Pascal Lavic: Follow the straight path if you want your spirit to separate quickly from your body of flesh.

Pascal Lavic: Live in the love of God. Pray.

Pascal Lavic: Then death, so terrible for some, will become gentler for you, because you'll know the life that's waiting for you.

Pascal Lavic: I died at sea. They waited for me a long time.

Pascal Lavic: Not being able to get free from my body was a terrible trial for me.

Pascal Lavic: That's why I need your prayers, because your faith can help bring me salvation.

Pascal Lavic: You can pray to God for me in the right way.

Pascal Lavic: I repent.

Pascal Lavic: I hope God will forgive me.

Pascal Lavic: It was on August 6 that my body was found.

Pascal Lavic: I was a poor seafaring man, and I died a long time ago.

Pascal Lavic: Pray for me.

Medium: Where was your body found?

Pascal Lavic: Near here.

Background: A newspaper report later confirmed the details. The medium had known nothing about the discovery beforehand.

Background: On August 12, while the case was being discussed in the group where he had appeared, Pascal Lavic spoke again.

Pascal Lavic: I really am Pascal Lavic, and I need your prayers.

Pascal Lavic: You can do me good, because the trial I went through was very terrible.

Pascal Lavic: The separation of my spirit from my body only happened after I remembered my faults.

Pascal Lavic: And even then, I wasn't completely separated from it, but followed it for a long time while it was tossed around by the waves.

Pascal Lavic: Beg God to forgive me.

Pascal Lavic: Ask Him to give me rest.

Pascal Lavic: Pray for me, I beg you.

Pascal Lavic: Let this terrible end of an unhappy life be a great lesson for all of you.

Pascal Lavic: Think about the life to come, and never fail to ask God to have pity on you.

Pascal Lavic: Pray for me.

Pascal Lavic: Pray that God may have pity on me.
