Auguste Michel was young, rich, smart, charming, and naturally kind, but he lived for pleasure, luxury, vanity, and physical indulgence. He committed no great crime, yet he did no real good. He died in an accident after being thrown from a carriage.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Auguste Michel: I've barely been separated from my body, so it's hard for me to speak to you.

Auguste Michel: The terrible fall that killed my body has thrown my spirit into deep confusion.

Auguste Michel: I'm anxious about what's going to happen to me, and that uncertainty is very painful.

Auguste Michel: The dreadful suffering my body felt is nothing compared with the confusion I'm in now.

Auguste Michel: Pray for me, that God may forgive me.

Auguste Michel: Oh, what misery.

Auguste Michel: Oh God, have pity on me.

Auguste Michel: What misery.

Auguste Michel: Farewell.

Ten days later he returned, still attached to his body and still confused.

Auguste Michel: I came to you the other day, but I could hardly speak. Even now it's still hard for me.

Auguste Michel: You're the only medium through whom I can ask for prayer, so God's mercy may deliver me from the confusion I'm in.

Auguste Michel: Why do I still suffer when my body doesn't suffer anymore?

Auguste Michel: Why does this horrible pain, this terrible anguish, still cling to me?

Auguste Michel: Pray, oh, pray for me, that God may give me rest.

Auguste Michel: What terrible uncertainty.

Auguste Michel: I'm still attached to my body.

Auguste Michel: I can't understand where I am.

Auguste Michel: My body is there. Why am I still there too?

Auguste Michel: Come and pray over it, so I can be released from its cruel hold.

Auguste Michel: Surely God will forgive me.

Auguste Michel: I see spirits near you. It's with their help that I can speak to you.

Auguste Michel: Pray for me.

On April 6, he again begged the medium to go where his body was lying and pray there. The medium delayed because he didn't understand why the request mattered so much.

On May 11, the medium finally went to the grave and received this message there.

Auguste Michel: I was waiting for you.

Auguste Michel: I'd been longing for the moment when you'd come to the place where my spirit seems fixed to its bodily covering, to beg the God of mercy to calm my suffering.

Auguste Michel: Your prayers can do me good.

Auguste Michel: I beg you, don't stop praying for me.

Auguste Michel: I can now see how far my life was from what it should've been.

Auguste Michel: I see the faults I committed.

Auguste Michel: I was of no use while I was in the world.

Auguste Michel: I made no good use of my abilities.

Auguste Michel: My fortune only served my passions, my love of luxury, and my vanity.

Auguste Michel: I thought only of physical pleasures, and not of my soul.

Auguste Michel: Will the pity of God ever reach me, an unhappy spirit still suffering for the faults of life on Earth?

Auguste Michel: Pray that He may forgive me, and that I may be freed from the pain I still feel.

Auguste Michel: Thank you for coming here to pray over me.

By June, he was calmer and thinking more clearly.

Auguste Michel: I'm able to speak to you, and I thank God for letting me do it.

Auguste Michel: I see my faults, and I hope God will forgive me.

Auguste Michel: Hold to the belief that guides you all your life, because that way you'll gain a peace I still haven't received.

Auguste Michel: Thank you for your prayers. I will come to you again.

In a later communication, he spoke more freely and warned others about the life he had lived.

Auguste Michel: I'm less unhappy now, because I no longer feel the chain that held me to my body.

Auguste Michel: At last I am free.

Auguste Michel: But my atonement isn't finished.

Auguste Michel: I have to make up for lost time if I don't want to drag out my suffering.

Auguste Michel: I trust that God will see that my repentance is sincere and grant me His forgiveness.

Auguste Michel: I beg you, keep praying for me.

Auguste Michel: Men, my brothers, I lived only for myself. Now I'm paying for that wrong, and I suffer.

Auguste Michel: May God give you the grace to avoid the thorns that are tearing me apart.

Auguste Michel: Walk the broad road of holiness, and pray for me, because I made bad use of the things God lends to His creatures.

Auguste Michel: Anyone who sacrifices intelligence and higher feelings to animal instincts lowers himself toward the animals.

Auguste Michel: A person should use with moderation the property he's only a keeper of.

Auguste Michel: He should train himself to live for the eternity waiting for him, and in that way detach himself from material pleasures.

Auguste Michel: Food should have no purpose but to sustain life.

Auguste Michel: Luxury should always stay below the real needs of one's position.

Auguste Michel: Tastes, and even natural tendencies, should be governed by reason.

Auguste Michel: Without that control over our lower nature, we drag ourselves down instead of purifying ourselves.

Auguste Michel: Human passions are a tight bond that cuts into the flesh.

Auguste Michel: Be careful not to tighten it.

Auguste Michel: Live, but don't live in excess.

Auguste Michel: You don't know what that kind of excess costs when we return to the homeland of the soul.

Auguste Michel: Earthly passions strip us of everything before they leave us, and we arrive before God naked, completely naked.

Auguste Michel: So free yourselves from those passions, and clothe yourselves with good deeds.

Auguste Michel: They'll help you cross the distance that separates you from eternity.

Auguste Michel: They'll cover your human weakness with a shining mantle.

Auguste Michel: Clothe yourselves with charity and love, divine garments that no one can take from you.

The guide then explained the spiritual state of people who live only for the senses.

Mediums’ Guide: This spirit is on the right road, because along with repentance he now gives good advice about the dangers of the path he once followed.

Mediums’ Guide: Admitting one's faults is already meaningful, and it's the first step toward reform.

Mediums’ Guide: For that reason, his condition, though not happy yet, is no longer that of a suffering spirit in the strict sense.

Mediums’ Guide: But do you want to know the condition of spirits whose life on Earth was entirely sensual and never stirred the spirit to any activity except inventing new bodily pleasures?

Mediums’ Guide: The influence of matter follows them beyond the grave.

Mediums’ Guide: Their appetites remain after death, but their outlook is as narrow as it was on Earth, and they search in vain for ways to satisfy them.

Mediums’ Guide: Since they never developed mental or moral pleasures, their soul wanders through space, which is emptiness to them, without purpose and without hope.

Mediums’ Guide: They're seized by the anxiety of someone who sees nothing ahead but a boundless desert.

Mediums’ Guide: The emptiness of their inner life on Earth naturally becomes the emptiness of the spirit's life after death.

Mediums’ Guide: No longer able to satisfy the body, and still unable to satisfy the soul, they fall into crushing weariness and can't see any end to it.

Mediums’ Guide: To escape it, they would gladly accept total destruction.

Mediums’ Guide: But total destruction does not exist.

Mediums’ Guide: They may destroy the body, but they cannot destroy the soul.

Mediums’ Guide: So they're forced to keep living through all that mental torture until, worn down by exhaustion, they finally decide to turn toward God.
