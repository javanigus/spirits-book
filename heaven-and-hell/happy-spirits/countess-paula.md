Background: Countess Paula was young, beautiful, wealthy, and born into high social rank. Yet what made her truly admired was not her status, but the goodness of her heart. She was gentle, humble, forgiving, and kind to everyone around her. She never spread gossip or slander, never humiliated those beneath her socially, and never displayed pride or arrogance.

Background: She treated servants, workers, and tradespeople with unusual fairness and respect. She believed that anyone who worked for a living deserved prompt payment, and she would have felt ashamed if someone had needed to wait for wages or payment because of her.

Background: Her charity was immense, but discreet. She helped the suffering quietly and without seeking praise. She possessed a remarkable ability to discover hidden misery — especially among people trying desperately to conceal their poverty from the world — and she helped them with such delicacy that their dignity remained intact.

Background: Although her social position required a luxurious lifestyle, she managed her household with wisdom and avoided waste or vanity. Because of this, she was able to devote a large part of her fortune to helping the poor.

Background: Long before her death, she had already set aside a portion of her wealth exclusively for charitable works. In this way, she successfully balanced the duties of her social rank with her constant desire to do good.

Background: She died in 1851 at the age of thirty-six. Her death caused widespread sorrow because everyone who knew her loved and respected her. Twelve years later, after one of her relatives became a Spiritist, her spirit was evoked and gave the following communication.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Countess Paula: You are correct in believing me to be happy.

Countess Paula: I'm happy beyond the power of words to express.

Countess Paula: And yet... I'm still far from the summit.

Countess Paula: I'm incomparably happier than I was on Earth, even though I was considered one of the happiest of earthly women.

Countess Paula: During my life on Earth, I don't remember ever experiencing any truly deep sorrow.

Countess Paula: I possessed youth, health, fortune, admiration, and everything human beings usually call happiness.

Countess Paula: But what are such things compared with the happiness we experience here?

Countess Paula: What are your most magnificent celebrations, where guests display their finest clothing...

Countess Paula: Compared with the gatherings of spirits clothed in radiant light that your earthly eyes could not endure?

Countess Paula: What are your palaces, your golden salons, and your beautiful gardens...

Countess Paula: Compared with the aerial dwellings and immense regions of space filled with colors that make the rainbow itself appear pale?

Countess Paula: What are your slow earthly journeys compared with our flights through infinity, swifter than lightning?

Countess Paula: What are your narrow and cloudy horizons compared with the grand spectacle of countless worlds moving through the endless universe beneath the hand of God?

Countess Paula: How harsh and empty your most beautiful music seems compared with the heavenly harmony vibrating through all the spiritual elements surrounding us.

Countess Paula: How dull your greatest earthly pleasures seem compared with the indescribable happiness constantly filling our entire being.

Countess Paula: Here, everything breathes love, trust, and sincerity.

Countess Paula: Everywhere we find loving hearts.

Countess Paula: Everywhere we meet friends.

Countess Paula: Nowhere do we encounter envy or jealousy.

Countess Paula: Such, my friend, is the world in which I now live.

Countess Paula: And you too will certainly reach it if you follow the right path.

Countess Paula: But don't imagine that happiness here becomes monotonous.

Countess Paula: It's not an endless concert.

Countess Paula: Or an endless festival.

Countess Paula: Or some lifeless state of contemplation.

Countess Paula: No.

Countess Paula: It is movement, life, and activity.

Countess Paula: Our occupations involve no fatigue.

Countess Paula: Yet they constantly bring new experiences, new emotions, and endless variety.

Countess Paula: Each of us has missions to fulfill.

Countess Paula: Spirits to assist.

Countess Paula: Friends on Earth to visit.

Countess Paula: Portions of nature’s great machinery to direct.

Countess Paula: And suffering spirits of lower condition to comfort.

Countess Paula: We come and go, not merely from one street to another, but from one world to another.

Countess Paula: We gather together and separate again.

Countess Paula: We arrange meetings.

Countess Paula: We recount our work.

Countess Paula: We congratulate one another for successful efforts.

Countess Paula: We consult together.

Countess Paula: And we help one another whenever difficulties arise.

Countess Paula: I assure you... no spirit here has time to feel bored even for a single moment.

Countess Paula: At present, our greatest subject of interest is the Earth.

Countess Paula: An immense movement is now taking place among spirits.

Countess Paula: Great multitudes are arriving from every direction to participate in the transformation of your world.

Countess Paula: They resemble armies of workers reclaiming a wild forest under the guidance of experienced leaders.

Countess Paula: Some uproot old trees and ancient roots.

Countess Paula: Others clear away stones and debris.

Countess Paula: Others plow, dig, sow, and plant.

Countess Paula: Meanwhile, the leaders gather together in council and send messengers carrying instructions everywhere.

Countess Paula: The Earth must be regenerated within a determined time.

Countess Paula: The designs of God must be fulfilled.

Countess Paula: So, everyone is actively at work.

Countess Paula: And don't imagine that I remain merely a spectator.

Countess Paula: On the contrary, an important responsibility has been entrusted to me.

Countess Paula: And I strive with all my strength to fulfill it worthily.

Countess Paula: I did not attain my present condition without many struggles.

Countess Paula: Believe me... my final life on Earth alone, admirable though it may have appeared, would not have been enough to give me this elevation.

Countess Paula: During many previous lives, I voluntarily chose trials of labor and poverty in order to strengthen and purify my soul.

Countess Paula: I had the happiness of emerging victorious from those trials.

Countess Paula: But one final trial still remained before me.

Countess Paula: The most dangerous trial of all.

Countess Paula: The trial of wealth, comfort, and happiness without suffering.

Countess Paula: That was my greatest danger.

Countess Paula: Before accepting that dangerous trial, I wished to become strong enough not to fall beneath its temptations.

Countess Paula: The Divine Master saw my sincere intentions and granted me the help necessary to succeed.

Countess Paula: Many spirits, seduced by appearances, hastily choose the dangerous test of earthly prosperity.

Countess Paula: But being too weak and inexperienced, they are overcome by its temptations.

Countess Paula: Workers...

Countess Paula: I too once lived among you.

Countess Paula: I, who later became a noble lady, once earned my bread with the sweat of my brow just as you do.

Countess Paula: I endured privation, suffering, and harsh weather.

Countess Paula: And it was those sufferings that developed the strength of my soul.

Countess Paula: Without those earlier struggles, I might have failed during my final earthly trial.

Countess Paula: And such a failure would have cast me far backward.

Countess Paula: Like me, all of you will someday undergo the trial of worldly prosperity.

Countess Paula: But don't rush to ask for it too soon.

Countess Paula: And those among you who are already rich...

Countess Paula: Remember always that true and lasting wealth does not exist on Earth.

Countess Paula: Learn how to earn the blessing of God.
