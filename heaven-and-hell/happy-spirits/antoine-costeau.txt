Background: Antoine Costeau was a member of the Spiritist Society of Paris and was buried in September 1863 in the paupers’ section of the Montmartre Cemetery. He was a simple street worker who possessed very little materially, yet he was known for his deep goodness, humility, charity, and sincere faith in God and the future life.

Background: Even with his small income, he always found a way to help those poorer than himself. Spiritists believed that an expensive tomb meant nothing compared with the moral value of a righteous life, so the Society chose to use its limited funds to help the living rather than purchase a costly grave for the dead.

Background: At the cemetery, Mr. Canu, secretary of the Society and once a convinced materialist, spoke beside Costeau’s open grave. He said that a few years earlier many of them would have seen only annihilation in such a scene.

Background: Now, through Spiritism, they saw the grave as the end of a trial and the beginning of Costeau’s reward for his courage, patience, charity, and goodness. He asked Costeau to carry their gratitude to God, who had lifted them out of doubt.

Background: Instead of saying a hopeless farewell, they now said: may God bless you until we meet again. Be happy... and pray for us.

Background: Immediately after the speech, one of the Society’s mediums received a spontaneous communication from Costeau beside the open grave itself. Everyone present — including the grave-diggers — listened with deep emotion as the words of the deceased were read aloud beside his own body.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Antoine Costeau: Thank you, friends... thank you!

Antoine Costeau: My grave is not yet closed.

Antoine Costeau: In a few moments, the Earth will cover my mortal remains.

Antoine Costeau: But you know very well that my soul won't be buried beneath this dust.

Antoine Costeau: It will rise upward through space toward God.

Antoine Costeau: What a comfort it is to still be able to say, even after the breaking apart of the physical body:

Antoine Costeau: “No... I'm not dead.”

Antoine Costeau: “I'm alive with the true life... the eternal life.”

Antoine Costeau: The funeral of a poor man is not followed by crowds.

Antoine Costeau: No worldly display surrounds his grave.

Antoine Costeau: And yet, friends, believe me...

Antoine Costeau: An immense multitude is gathered around you right now.

Antoine Costeau: Good spirits have accompanied this humble coffin to its resting place.

Antoine Costeau: They came because you believe in God and honor His goodness.

Antoine Costeau: Oh no, my beloved wife... we don't die merely because the body breaks down.

Antoine Costeau: From now on, I will always remain near you.

Antoine Costeau: I will comfort you and help you endure the trial of my departure.

Antoine Costeau: Life will be difficult for you.

Antoine Costeau: But with your heart filled with thoughts of eternity and love for our Creator, earthly sorrows will seem of little importance.

Antoine Costeau: Relatives gathered around the beloved companion of my life...

Antoine Costeau: Give her your affection and respect.

Antoine Costeau: Be for her like brothers and sisters.

Antoine Costeau: Never forget that you must help one another during life on Earth if you wish to enter the peace of the spirit world.

Antoine Costeau: And you, Spiritists... friends... brothers...

Antoine Costeau: Thank you for coming to bid me farewell in this place of dust and mud.

Antoine Costeau: You know that my soul is immortal.

Antoine Costeau: You know that it still lives.

Antoine Costeau: And that it will often come to ask for your prayers.

Antoine Costeau: Don't refuse them to me.

Antoine Costeau: They will help me advance along the magnificent road you opened before me during life on Earth.

Antoine Costeau: Farewell, all of you gathered here.

Antoine Costeau: We will meet again somewhere far better than beside this grave.

Antoine Costeau: Happy spirits are calling me to go with them.

Antoine Costeau: Farewell!

Antoine Costeau: Pray for all who suffer.

Background: Three days later, Antoine Costeau was evoked again in a private gathering through another medium.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Antoine Costeau: Death is life.

Antoine Costeau: I'm only repeating what has already been said.

Antoine Costeau: But truly, there is nothing else to say, no matter how strongly materialists insist on remaining blind.

Antoine Costeau: Oh friends... what a glorious sight it is to see the Spiritist banner unfolding across the Earth.

Antoine Costeau: Proclaim the great revelation whose beginnings you have only barely started to witness.

Antoine Costeau: What magnificent light it brings to those willing to be enlightened...

Antoine Costeau: To those who have broken the chains of pride...

Antoine Costeau: And who dare openly proclaim their belief in God.

Antoine Costeau: Pray, dear brothers.

Antoine Costeau: Thank God for all the blessings He grants us.

Antoine Costeau: Poor human race...

Antoine Costeau: If only you could truly understand.

Antoine Costeau: But no... the time has not yet come for all humanity to fully receive the mercy of God and understand His divine will.

Antoine Costeau: It is through your luminous doctrine — this Science of Sciences — that human beings will eventually come to understanding.

Antoine Costeau: It is through its divine fire that hearts will be warmed again.

Antoine Costeau: Through it, the comforting flame of faith and hope will be rekindled.

Antoine Costeau: Through the beneficial influence of its revelations, masters and workers will finally become united in brotherhood.

Antoine Costeau: Because Spiritism explains human life in a way that reveals the true charity taught by the Divine Messiah.

Antoine Costeau: Reflect, my brothers, upon the immense happiness granted to you by being among the first initiated into this work of regeneration.

Antoine Costeau: Honor to you, friends!

Antoine Costeau: Continue your labor faithfully.

Antoine Costeau: And one day, like me, when you arrive in the spirit world, you too will say:

Antoine Costeau: “Death is life.”

Antoine Costeau: Or rather...

Antoine Costeau: Death is like a dream.

Antoine Costeau: A sort of nightmare lasting only a brief moment.

Antoine Costeau: And afterward, you awaken to find yourself surrounded by loving friends who welcome you with open arms and ease your arrival.

Antoine Costeau: My happiness was so immense that at first I could hardly believe God would grant me so many blessings for the little good I had accomplished on Earth.

Antoine Costeau: I truly thought I must be dreaming.

Antoine Costeau: And because I had sometimes dreamed during life on Earth that I was dead...

Antoine Costeau: For a few moments I feared I might be forced to return to the miserable body I had just left behind.

Antoine Costeau: But I quickly understood the truth of my situation.

Antoine Costeau: And I blessed the Supreme Master who allowed me to fulfill, on Earth, the duties required of those who sincerely wish to prepare for the future life.

Antoine Costeau: Yes indeed... I blessed and thanked God for this immense blessing.

Antoine Costeau: For The Spirits’ Book awakened within my soul the love of my Creator.

Antoine Costeau: Thank you, friends, for bringing me toward this truth.

Antoine Costeau: Tell our brothers and sisters that I'm often with our friend Sanson.

Antoine Costeau: Courage!

Antoine Costeau: Victory is assured for our doctrine.

Antoine Costeau: Happy are those who have taken part in the struggle.
