Samuel Philippe was known as a deeply honest and selfless man. He was never known to deliberately harm anyone or commit a wicked act. His devotion to his friends was limitless, and anyone who needed help could rely on him, even when helping them required personal sacrifice. He willingly endured hardship, exhaustion, and inconvenience in order to help others, and he did so naturally, without seeking praise or recognition. When people thanked him for his kindness, he was genuinely surprised that anyone considered his actions extraordinary.

Even people who treated him badly still received kindness from him. If someone repaid his generosity with ingratitude, he would calmly say, “It is not I who should be pitied... but they.”

Although highly intelligent and naturally gifted, his life on Earth was difficult and filled with heavy trials. He was one of those rare souls whose goodness remains mostly unnoticed by the world. Through Spiritism, he developed a deep faith in the future life and great acceptance of earthly suffering. He died in December 1862, at the age of fifty, after a long and painful illness. Several months later, his spirit was evoked.

Allan: Do you clearly remember your final moments on Earth?

Samuel Philippe: Perfectly.

Samuel Philippe: The memory returned to me little by little.

Samuel Philippe: At the moment of my departure, however, my thoughts were confused.

Allan: For our instruction, and because of the admiration we feel for your exemplary life, would you kindly describe how your passage from life on Earth into spirit life happened?

Allan: And would you also describe the condition in which you now find yourself?

Samuel Philippe: Gladly.

Samuel Philippe: This account will be useful not only to you, but also to myself.

Samuel Philippe: By turning my thoughts back toward the Earth, the comparison helps me better appreciate the goodness of the Creator.

Samuel Philippe: You know how full of sorrows my life was.

Samuel Philippe: But thank God... my courage never failed me during hardship.

Samuel Philippe: And now I rejoice that I endured my sufferings courageously.

Samuel Philippe: How much I would have lost had I given way to discouragement!

Samuel Philippe: I tremble at the thought that, through weakness, I might have lost the benefit of everything I endured and been forced to begin the lesson all over again.

Samuel Philippe: Oh friends... be fully convinced of this truth.

Samuel Philippe: Your future happiness depends upon it.

Samuel Philippe: No... a few years of suffering are not too high a price to pay for eternal happiness.

Samuel Philippe: If you could only understand how small a few short years are compared to eternity!

Samuel Philippe: If my last existence appears somewhat admirable to you, you would not have said the same of those that came before it.

Samuel Philippe: It was only through constant struggles against my evil tendencies that I became what I am now.

Samuel Philippe: In order to erase the last traces of my former faults, I needed to undergo these final trials... trials that I accepted willingly.

Samuel Philippe: The firmness of my determination gave me the strength to endure them without complaint.

Samuel Philippe: I now bless those trials.

Samuel Philippe: Through them, I broke with the past, which for me is now only a memory.

Samuel Philippe: And I can now look back with rightful satisfaction at the progress I have made.

Samuel Philippe: Oh... you who caused me suffering on Earth...

Samuel Philippe: You who were harsh and unkind to me...

Samuel Philippe: You who humiliated me and filled my cup with bitterness...

Samuel Philippe: You whose betrayal often reduced me to severe hardship...

Samuel Philippe: I not only forgive you...

Samuel Philippe: I thank you for everything you did.

Samuel Philippe: You little realized that while trying to harm me, you were actually doing me great good.

Samuel Philippe: I owe much of my present happiness to you.

Samuel Philippe: Because you gave me the opportunity to forgive... and to return good for evil.

Samuel Philippe: God placed you in my path in order to test my patience.

Samuel Philippe: And to exercise me in the most difficult form of charity... the love of one’s enemies.

Samuel Philippe: But don't be impatient with this side discussion.

Samuel Philippe: I now return to the questions you asked me.

Samuel Philippe: Although I suffered terribly during my final illness, I experienced no death struggle.

Samuel Philippe: Death came upon me like sleep.

Samuel Philippe: There was no effort... no shock.

Samuel Philippe: Because I had no fear of the future, I did not cling to life on Earth.

Samuel Philippe: So, I had no need to struggle against the separation from the body.

Samuel Philippe: The separation occurred without effort, without pain... and even without my awareness.

Samuel Philippe: I don't know how long this kind of sleep lasted.

Samuel Philippe: But it lasted only a short time.

Samuel Philippe: My awakening was calm and delightful compared to my previous condition.

Samuel Philippe: I no longer felt pain.

Samuel Philippe: And I rejoiced in my freedom.

Samuel Philippe: I wanted to rise and walk.

Samuel Philippe: But a pleasant heaviness held me motionless.

Samuel Philippe: It was not unpleasant at all.

Samuel Philippe: On the contrary... it felt soothing.

Samuel Philippe: I surrendered myself to it with a kind of enjoyment.

Samuel Philippe: I did not yet try to understand my situation.

Samuel Philippe: Yet I did not doubt that I had left the Earth.

Samuel Philippe: Everything around me seemed dreamlike.

Samuel Philippe: I saw my wife and several friends kneeling in the room and weeping.

Samuel Philippe: I said to myself that they probably believed I was dead.

Samuel Philippe: I wanted to tell them they were mistaken.

Samuel Philippe: But I could not speak a single word.

Samuel Philippe: From this, I concluded that I truly must be dreaming.

Samuel Philippe: This impression became even stronger because I saw myself surrounded by several people I had loved who had long since died.

Samuel Philippe: I also saw others whom I did not recognize at first.

Samuel Philippe: They seemed to be watching over me and waiting for me to awaken.

Samuel Philippe: This state alternated between moments of clarity and moments of sleep-like unconsciousness.

Samuel Philippe: I would regain awareness of myself... and then lose it again.

Samuel Philippe: Gradually, my thoughts became clearer.

Samuel Philippe: The light I had first seen through a kind of fog became brighter.

Samuel Philippe: Little by little, I recovered full awareness.

Samuel Philippe: Then I understood that I no longer belonged to the earthly world.

Samuel Philippe: If I had not known Spiritism, this illusion would probably have lasted much longer.

Samuel Philippe: My physical body had not yet been buried.

Samuel Philippe: I looked upon it with a kind of sympathetic contempt.

Samuel Philippe: I congratulated myself on being rid of it.

Samuel Philippe: I was so happy to be free.

Samuel Philippe: I breathed easily, like someone escaping from a foul and suffocating atmosphere.

Samuel Philippe: An impossible-to-describe happiness filled my entire being.

Samuel Philippe: The presence of those I had once loved filled me with joy.

Samuel Philippe: I was not at all surprised to see them.

Samuel Philippe: It seemed perfectly natural.

Samuel Philippe: It felt as though I had simply found them again after a long journey.

Samuel Philippe: One thing greatly surprised me at first.

Samuel Philippe: We understood one another without speaking.

Samuel Philippe: Our thoughts were communicated instantly through a kind of spiritual connection.

Samuel Philippe: Still, I was not yet completely freed from earthly ideas.

Samuel Philippe: The memory of everything I had suffered returned from time to time to my mind.

Samuel Philippe: It seemed to happen so that I would appreciate even more the happiness of my new condition.

Samuel Philippe: I had suffered greatly physically.

Samuel Philippe: But I had suffered even more emotionally.

Samuel Philippe: I had been the target of hostility.

Samuel Philippe: I had endured countless worries and anxieties that sometimes torment us more than serious misfortunes because they keep us in constant uneasiness.

Samuel Philippe: The impression left by those worries had not completely disappeared.

Samuel Philippe: At times, I asked myself whether I was truly free from them.

Samuel Philippe: Sometimes it seemed as though I still heard certain unpleasant voices.

Samuel Philippe: I feared the return of the troubles that had once tormented me so often.

Samuel Philippe: In spite of myself, I trembled.

Samuel Philippe: I almost touched myself to make certain I was not dreaming.

Samuel Philippe: And when I finally became certain that I was truly freed from the troubles of life on Earth, it felt as though an enormous burden had been lifted from me.

Samuel Philippe: “So it is really true,” I exclaimed.

Samuel Philippe: “I'm finally delivered from the worries that torment human life!”

Samuel Philippe: And I thanked God with the deepest thankfulness for this freedom.

Samuel Philippe: I felt like a poor man who suddenly inherits immense wealth but at first can't believe the change in his condition and still fears returning to poverty.

Samuel Philippe: Ah... if people could truly understand the nature of the future life...

Samuel Philippe: What strength and courage they would gain during hardship from the certainty of its reality!

Samuel Philippe: What efforts they would make while on Earth to secure the happiness God has prepared for His obedient children!

Samuel Philippe: They would then understand how worthless earthly pleasures truly are compared to the joys of the future life... joys to which they give so little thought.

Allan: Has the spirit world... which now seems so new to you and beside which our world appears so unimportant... caused you to forget the family and friends you left behind on Earth?

Samuel Philippe: If I could forget them, I would be undeserving of the happiness I now enjoy.

Samuel Philippe: God does not reward selfishness.

Samuel Philippe: He punishes it.

Samuel Philippe: The world where I now live may make me unconcerned with earthly things...

Samuel Philippe: But not with the spirits still living on Earth.

Samuel Philippe: Only among human beings do the fortunate forget those who suffer.

Samuel Philippe: I often return to visit those with whom I was connected during life on Earth.

Samuel Philippe: I rejoice in their affectionate remembrance of me.

Samuel Philippe: Their thoughts attract me toward them.

Samuel Philippe: I join them when they gather together.

Samuel Philippe: I share their joys and I grieve at their sorrows.

Samuel Philippe: But my sympathy is not the anxious suffering of human sadness...

Samuel Philippe: Because I know their troubles are temporary and ultimately for their own good.

Samuel Philippe: I rejoice in knowing that they too will someday arrive in this happy place where suffering is unknown.

Samuel Philippe: Above all, I devote myself to helping them become deserving of this place.

Samuel Philippe: By every means in my power, I try to inspire good thoughts within them.

Samuel Philippe: And especially, I try to strengthen them in acceptance of the Divine will.

Samuel Philippe: My greatest sorrow is seeing them delay their own happiness through lack of courage, through complaining, through doubting the future, or through wrong actions.

Samuel Philippe: I try to turn them away from the wrong path.

Samuel Philippe: If I succeed, it brings great joy to me and to all our friends here.

Samuel Philippe: And if I fail, I sadly say to myself:

Samuel Philippe: “This is another delay for them.”

Samuel Philippe: But I comfort myself with the thought that it is not forever.

Samuel Philippe: In time... they will all reach the goal.
