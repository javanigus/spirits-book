Bordeaux, February 11, 1861. Sixdeniers was described as an excellent and kind-hearted man who died in an accident by drowning. He had been personally known by the medium during his life on Earth. After his death, his spirit was evoked in order to describe what he experienced after passing into the spirit world.

Allan: Can you give me any details concerning your death?

Sixdeniers: About what happened after the drowning... yes.

Allan: Why not before?

Sixdeniers: Because you already know those details.

The medium indeed already knew the circumstances of his death.

Allan: Please describe what you felt after your death.

Sixdeniers: It was a long time before I recovered consciousness.

Sixdeniers: But with the grace of God and the help of the friends around me, when the light finally became visible to me, I was flooded by it.

Sixdeniers: Be hopeful.

Sixdeniers: When you arrive here, you will find far more than you expected.

Sixdeniers: There is nothing material here.

Sixdeniers: Everything is perceived through senses that remain hidden from you during life on Earth.

Sixdeniers: You perceive things that can't be seen by the eyes or touched by the hands.

Sixdeniers: Do you understand what I mean?

Sixdeniers: It is a wonder experienced by the spirit itself... something beyond your power to fully understand.

Sixdeniers: No words can truly explain it.

Sixdeniers: It can only be felt by the soul.

Sixdeniers: My awakening was very happy.

Sixdeniers: Life on Earth now seems to me like one of those dreams that can only be described as a nightmare.

Sixdeniers: Imagine dreaming that you are locked inside a filthy dungeon.

Sixdeniers: Imagine your body being devoured by worms that gnaw into your bones.

Sixdeniers: Imagine yourself suspended above a furnace of fire.

Sixdeniers: Imagine your mouth burning with thirst and unable to find even a breath of fresh air.

Sixdeniers: Imagine your terrified spirit surrounded by monsters ready to devour you.

Sixdeniers: Imagine every horrible and terrifying thing the worst nightmare could invent.

Sixdeniers: And then imagine being instantly transported into a paradise of delight.

Sixdeniers: Imagine waking from that nightmare and finding yourself surrounded by all those you once loved and mourned.

Sixdeniers: Imagine seeing their beloved faces smiling at you joyfully.

Sixdeniers: Imagine breathing the most wonderful fragrances.

Sixdeniers: Imagine cooling your burning throat with living water.

Sixdeniers: Imagine yourself carried upward through infinite space as lightly as a flower carried by the breeze.

Sixdeniers: Imagine feeling yourself surrounded by Infinite Love just as a baby is surrounded by the love of its mother.

Sixdeniers: Imagine all of this...

Sixdeniers: And even then, you would still have only a faint idea of the transition that follows death.

Sixdeniers: I have tried, through these comparisons, to explain the happiness awaiting human beings after death.

Sixdeniers: But it is something impossible to fully describe.

Sixdeniers: Can the infinity of the sky be explained to a blind cripple whose eyes have never seen light and whose limbs have never carried him beyond his prison?

Sixdeniers: If I wished to give you one idea of the happiness of eternity, I would say only this:

Sixdeniers: Love.

Sixdeniers: Because only love can give you a glimpse of that happiness.

Sixdeniers: And true love can't exist together with selfishness.

Allan: Was your condition immediately happy when you entered the spirit world?

Sixdeniers: No.

Sixdeniers: I still had to pay the debt of my life on Earth.

Sixdeniers: In my heart, I had sensed the existence of a future spiritual life.

Sixdeniers: But I did not possess an active faith in that future.

Sixdeniers: So, I had to atone for my indifference toward my Creator.

Sixdeniers: But God’s mercy took into account the little good I had been able to do.

Sixdeniers: It also took into account the sufferings I had endured with acceptance, despite the pain they caused me.

Sixdeniers: Divine Justice weighs all things according to laws human beings can't fully understand.

Sixdeniers: And that Justice weighed my merits with such kindness and love that my faults were quickly erased.

Allan: Will you give me news of your daughter?

His daughter had died four or five years before him.

Sixdeniers: She is fulfilling a mission on Earth.

Allan: Is she happy in this reincarnation?

Allan: I hope my question is not indiscreet.

Sixdeniers: I can't see it as indiscreet.

Sixdeniers: I see your thoughts before me as clearly as a picture.

Sixdeniers: No... her life on Earth is not happy.

Sixdeniers: On the contrary, she must endure many of the troubles of your world.

Sixdeniers: But through her example, she will demonstrate the noble virtues about which people so often speak without practicing them.

Sixdeniers: I will help her.

Sixdeniers: She won't have great difficulty overcoming the obstacles before her.

Sixdeniers: Her present life is not an atonement... but a mission.

Sixdeniers: Be at peace concerning her.

Sixdeniers: And accept my thanks for your kind remembrance.

At this moment, the medium suddenly experienced difficulty writing and said aloud:

Medium: If this is a suffering spirit trying to take control of my hand, I ask that spirit to write its name.

Valeria: I'm one who is very unhappy.

Allan: Please tell me your name.

Valeria: Valeria.

Allan: Will you tell me what caused your punishment?

Valeria: No.

Allan: Do you repent of your wrongdoing?

Valeria: You can already see that I do.

Allan: Who brought you here?

Valeria: Sixdeniers.

Allan: For what purpose did he bring you here?

Valeria: So that you may help me.

Allan: Was it you who prevented me from writing just now?

Valeria: He allowed me to take his place.

Allan: What connection exists between you?

Valeria: He guides me.

Allan: Ask him to join in the prayer we are about to offer for you.

After the prayer, Sixdeniers again took control of the medium’s hand.

Sixdeniers: Thank you for her.

Sixdeniers: You understood what she needs.

Sixdeniers: Continue thinking of her.

Allan: Do you guide many suffering spirits?

Sixdeniers: No.

Sixdeniers: But as soon as we bring one back onto the right path, we take another in hand.

Sixdeniers: Even so, we don't lose sight of those we previously helped.

Allan: How can you possibly oversee so many spirits over such immense periods of time?

Sixdeniers: Those whom we guide back toward goodness gradually become purified and advance.

Sixdeniers: They then require less help from us.

Sixdeniers: And while helping them rise, we ourselves also rise.

Sixdeniers: As we rise, our abilities increase.

Sixdeniers: And our influence spreads more widely according to our degree of purity.

Allan Kardec then adds an observation.

Lower spirits are therefore helped by higher spirits whose mission is to guide them toward improvement.

This responsibility does not belong only to people living on Earth, although people on Earth should also participate in it because doing so helps their own spiritual progress.

When a lower spirit interrupts a communication, as happened in this case, the interruption is not always caused by bad intentions.

Higher spirits sometimes allow such interruptions as a test of the medium’s patience, or so that the medium may help improve the suffering spirit causing the interruption.

The more stubborn the obsession becomes, the greater the spirit’s need for help often is.

So, it is wrong to reject such spirits.

Instead, they should be treated like beggars in need of charity.

One should say:

“Here is an unhappy spirit sent to me by higher spirits so that I may help educate and improve him.”

“If I succeed, I will rejoice at having guided a wandering soul back toward goodness and shortened its suffering.”

The task is often difficult.

It would certainly be more pleasant to receive only beautiful communications from elevated spirits.

But by seeking only our own satisfaction and turning away from opportunities to do good, we would not deserve the protection of higher spirits.
