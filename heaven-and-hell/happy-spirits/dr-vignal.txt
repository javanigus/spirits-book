Background: Dr. Vignal was one of the earliest members of the Spiritist Society of Paris. He died on March 27th, 1865. Before his funeral, a highly spiritually aware somnambulist was asked to observe him spiritually and describe what she perceived during the separation of his spirit from the body.

Narration: The somnambulist speaks while observing spiritually...

Medium: I see a body in which an extraordinary process is taking place.

Medium: There is a mass whose every particle is moving.

Medium: Something within it is trying to detach itself.

Medium: But it struggles against the resistance still binding it to the body.

Medium: I can't yet distinguish any clearly formed spirit-body.

Background: Four days later, on March 31st, the Spiritist Society of Paris formally evoked Dr. Vignal.

Allan: Dear Dr. Vignal, all your old friends at the Society of Paris remember you affectionately.

Allan: I especially am very happy to call you here.

Allan: We would be grateful if you are able and willing to speak with us.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Dr. Vignal: Dear friends... and you, my dear and worthy teacher...

Dr. Vignal: Your remembrance and sympathy bring me great happiness.

Dr. Vignal: If I'm able to come among you today and participate freely in this gathering without the chains of the physical body...

Dr. Vignal: It is thanks to your kind thoughts and the assistance provided by your prayers.

Dr. Vignal: As my young secretary observed earlier, I have spent the entire evening impatiently wishing to communicate with you.

Dr. Vignal: I have had to exercise great self-control not to rush immediately into speaking.

Dr. Vignal: The discussions you were holding interested me greatly and helped make the wait less difficult.

Dr. Vignal: Forgive this impatience, dear friends.

Dr. Vignal: My gratitude wished to express itself immediately.

Allan: Please tell us how you now find yourself in the spirit world.

Allan: Describe your separation from the body.

Allan: What sensations did you experience?

Allan: And how long did it take before you regained full awareness?

Dr. Vignal: I'm happy to tell you that my experience has fully confirmed the teachings of our comforting and enlightening doctrine.

Dr. Vignal: I'm happy.

Dr. Vignal: Yes... truly happy.

Dr. Vignal: Because I can now clearly see, without obstacle, the future development of both Science and Spiritist Philosophy.

Dr. Vignal: But let us set aside those subjects for today.

Dr. Vignal: I will return another time to discuss them further.

Dr. Vignal: I already know that my visits will give you pleasure, just as your company gives pleasure to me.

Dr. Vignal: The separation from the body happened quickly.

Dr. Vignal: More quickly than I could have hoped, considering how small my merits are.

Dr. Vignal: Your prayers greatly assisted me during this process.

Dr. Vignal: And the somnambulist gave such an accurate description of the separation that I have little to add.

Dr. Vignal: It felt like an irregular oscillation.

Dr. Vignal: A kind of pulling in two opposite directions.

Dr. Vignal: At last, the spirit triumphed.

Dr. Vignal: And here I'm.

Dr. Vignal: I completely freed myself from my body only at the moment it was lowered into the grave.

Dr. Vignal: Then I returned here with all of you.

Allan: What did you think of the funeral ceremony?

Dr. Vignal: I considered it my duty to attend it.

Allan: At that moment, were you detached enough from your body to observe the ceremony clearly?

Allan: And did the prayers I offered for you reach you?

Dr. Vignal: Yes.

Dr. Vignal: I returned from the cemetery together with all of you.

Dr. Vignal: I left behind my old chrysalis completely separated from me.

Dr. Vignal: You know that earthly things mattered little to me.

Dr. Vignal: My thoughts were directed mainly toward spiritual life and toward God.

Allan: Do you remember that five years ago, while you were still alive, we used you as the subject of a Spiritist experiment?

Allan: During that experiment, your spirit partially detached from your body and communicated with us.

Allan: Can you explain the difference between that experience and your present state?

Dr. Vignal: Certainly, I remember that experiment.

Dr. Vignal: But what an immense difference exists between that state and my present condition.

Dr. Vignal: At that time, the rigid network of matter still held me tightly.

Dr. Vignal: I longed to separate myself more completely from the body...

Dr. Vignal: But I could not.

Dr. Vignal: Now I'm entirely free.

Dr. Vignal: An immense field — the field of the unknown — now opens before me.

Dr. Vignal: And with your help, together with the help of the good spirits to whom I entrust myself...

Dr. Vignal: I hope to advance rapidly.

Dr. Vignal: I hope to acquire, as soon as possible, the sentiments we must possess and the actions we must practice in order to pass through the narrow road of earthly trials and become worthy of the worlds of reward.

Dr. Vignal: What majesty...

Dr. Vignal: What grandeur...

Dr. Vignal: We are filled almost with awe when, weak as we still are, we try to gaze upon the sublime splendors now before us.

Allan: Once again, I tell you that we would be very happy to continue these conversations whenever you desire to return.

Dr. Vignal: I have done my best to answer your questions.

Dr. Vignal: But don't ask too much, at first, from your faithful disciple.

Dr. Vignal: I'm not yet completely freed from earthly influences.

Dr. Vignal: I would gladly continue speaking with you.

Dr. Vignal: But my guide is trying to moderate my enthusiasm.

Dr. Vignal: And I trust too deeply in his wisdom and kindness not to follow his advice, even though I regret ending this conversation.

Dr. Vignal: I take comfort in knowing that I will often return invisibly to your spiritual gatherings.

Dr. Vignal: I love you all.

Dr. Vignal: And I will come back.

Dr. Vignal: But now I give my place to spirits more advanced than myself, who kindly allowed me to pour out the flood of thoughts I longed to express.

Dr. Vignal: For now, I withdraw.

Dr. Vignal: I thank you for calling me here.

Dr. Vignal: And I also thank the spirit who kindly yielded his place to me...

Dr. Vignal: The spirit who, while on Earth, bore the illustrious name of Pascal.

Dr. Vignal: He who was, and always will remain, your devoted disciple.
