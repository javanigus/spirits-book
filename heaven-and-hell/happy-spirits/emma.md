Background: Emma was a beautiful and talented young actress and dancer who died after her dress accidentally caught fire during a stage performance. She endured terrible physical suffering before her death, yet faced it with patience, prayer, and acceptance. Shortly after her passing in 1863, her spirit spontaneously communicated at the Spiritist Society of Paris before anyone had formally evoked her.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Emma: Here I'm again upon the stage of the world.

Emma: I who believed myself buried forever beneath the veil of my youth and innocence.

Emma: According to my Catholic belief, I used to say to myself:

Emma: “The fire of Earth will save me from the fire of Hell.”

Emma: And although I did not dare hope for the splendors of Paradise...

Emma: During the terrible suffering caused by my accident, my soul took refuge in the thought that I was enduring beforehand the sufferings of Purgatory.

Emma: So I prayed.

Emma: I suffered.

Emma: And I wept with patience and acceptance.

Emma: But who gave my weakness the strength to endure such agony?

Emma: Who leaned over my bed during those endless nights of fever and sleepless torture?

Emma: Who cooled my burning lips?

Emma: It was you, my Guardian Angel.

Emma: You whose radiant purity surrounded me with blessing.

Emma: And it was also you, dear spirit-friends, who gathered around me whispering words of hope and love into my ears.

Emma: The flames that consumed my fragile earthly body also burned away every attachment my soul still held toward earthly things.

Emma: So, when I died, I was already living the true life of the spirit.

Emma: I experienced none of the confusion that usually follows death.

Emma: Instead, I entered immediately, calm and fully aware, into the radiant light surrounding those who maintain faith and hope amid suffering and trial.

Emma: The thought of my mother...

Emma: My beloved mother...

Emma: Was the final earthly image to vibrate within my soul.

Emma: How deeply I wish she would become a Spiritist.

Emma: I fell from the tree of life on Earth like a fruit ripened before its proper season.

Emma: I had scarcely begun to feel the dangerous temptations of vanity that so often ruin those carried away by success, youth, and beauty.

Emma: I bless the flames.

Emma: I bless my suffering.

Emma: I bless the trial that served as my atonement.

Emma: Like the delicate threads floating through autumn air, I now drift light and radiant through the luminous currents surrounding me.

Emma: And the jewels crowning my forehead are no longer lifeless diamonds from your earthly world...

Emma: But the living brilliance of a purified soul.

Background: The following day, at a Spiritist gathering in Havre, Emma spontaneously communicated again.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Emma: Those who suffer on Earth are rewarded in the next life.

Emma: God is perfectly just and merciful toward those who suffer in life on Earth.

Emma: The happiness granted in the spirit world is so pure and complete...

Emma: That if people truly understood it, no one would fear suffering or death.

Emma: If only human beings could glimpse the purposes of the Creator.

Emma: The Earth is a place of trials.

Emma: Often those trials are severe.

Emma: Often its sorrows are extremely painful.

Emma: But let those who suffer remain patient.

Emma: Let them bow before the will of God when, in His mercy, He allows them to carry a heavy burden.

Emma: When, after great suffering, God calls them back to Himself...

Emma: They will then understand how small earthly pains truly were compared with the reward awaiting them...

Emma: Provided they endured without complaint or bitterness.

Emma: I left the Earth very young.

Emma: God forgave me.

Emma: And He granted me the life reserved for those who respected His divine will.

Emma: Adore God in all things.

Emma: Love Him with all your heart.

Emma: And above all, pray to Him with unwavering trust.

Emma: Prayer is your true support during life on Earth.

Emma: It is your hope.

Emma: And your safety.
