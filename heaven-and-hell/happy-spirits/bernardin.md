Bordeaux, April 1862. Bernardin was a spirit who said he had been forgotten for many centuries. During his life on Earth, he lived in poverty, humiliation, and constant suffering. He belonged to the lower servant class during a brutal historical period when workers and servants were often treated with cruelty and injustice. Yet despite everything he endured, he kept faith in God and bore his trials without bitterness. After his death, he communicated in order to encourage others facing suffering and hardship.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Bernardin: I'm a spirit who has been forgotten for many centuries.

Bernardin: I lived on Earth in poverty and disgrace.

Bernardin: I worked without rest each day simply to earn enough bread for my family.

Bernardin: But I loved the One whom I regarded as my true Master.

Bernardin: And when my suffering increased because of the man who ruled over me on Earth, I would say:

Bernardin: “My God... give me the strength to bear this burden without complaining.”

Bernardin: In this way, I was atoning for a painful past.

Bernardin: But when I finally emerged from that great trial, my true Master welcomed me into His peace.

Bernardin: And now my greatest desire is to tell all of you:

Bernardin: “Men... my brothers... whatever price you must pay for your purification, the happiness awaiting you will repay it abundantly.”

Bernardin: I had no fixed trade.

Bernardin: I belonged to a large family of children.

Bernardin: I worked for anyone willing to help me earn a living.

Bernardin: I was born during a time when servants were treated harshly and cruelly.

Bernardin: I endured every injustice...

Bernardin: Every forced labor...

Bernardin: Every exploitation and theft...

Bernardin: Everything the subordinates of my earthly master chose to inflict upon me.

Bernardin: I saw my wife dishonored.

Bernardin: I saw my daughters carried away and later abandoned.

Bernardin: And I could not even utter a complaint.

Bernardin: I saw my sons torn away from me and forced into wars of plunder and crime.

Bernardin: And later hanged for faults they had not committed.

Bernardin: Ah, friends... if only you knew all that I endured during that long and miserable life.

Bernardin: But I was sustained by the hope of a happiness that does not belong to the Earth.

Bernardin: And that hope was fulfilled.

Bernardin: So, I have the right to tell you:

Bernardin: “Brothers... courage, patience, acceptance.”

Bernardin: My child...

Bernardin: Preserve what I have written.

Bernardin: This is a practical lesson.

Bernardin: People listen much more carefully to someone who can say:

Bernardin: “I have suffered even more than you... and I endured it without complaining.”

Allan: During what period did you live on Earth?

Bernardin: From 1400 to 1460.

Allan: Have you had another life on Earth since then?

Bernardin: Yes.

Bernardin: I later lived among you as a missionary.

Bernardin: A missionary of faith.

Bernardin: Not the faith invented by men...

Bernardin: But the true and pure faith that comes from God.

Allan: As a spirit, do you now have occupations or responsibilities?

Bernardin: Can you imagine that spirits remain inactive?

Bernardin: Inactivity and uselessness would be torture for them.

Bernardin: My mission is to guide the workers within the great industrial cities toward Spiritism.

Bernardin: I inspire them with good thoughts.

Bernardin: And I try to neutralize the harmful suggestions that backward spirits attempt to place into their minds.
