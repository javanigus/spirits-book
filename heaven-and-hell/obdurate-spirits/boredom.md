Background: Bordeaux, 1862. An unnamed spirit came on his own and asked for prayers. He said he had been wandering in boredom for around one hundred and eighty years.
Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...
Allan: What made you ask for prayers?
Victim of Boredom: I am tired of wandering with no purpose.
Allan: Have you been in this state for long?
Victim of Boredom: About one hundred and eighty years.
Allan: What did you do on Earth?
Victim of Boredom: Nothing good.
Allan: What is your condition among spirits?
Victim of Boredom: I am among those who suffer from boredom.
Allan: But that is not really a separate class, is it?
Victim of Boredom: Everything among us forms a class. Every feeling gathers those who feel the same, and that likeness brings us together.
Allan: Why have you remained so long without advancing, if you were not condemned to this state as a punishment?
Victim of Boredom: I was condemned to suffer boredom. For us, that is a kind of suffering. Anything that is not joy becomes suffering.
Allan: Have you been forced to remain wandering against your will?
Victim of Boredom: That question could only be answered by causes too subtle for your earthly mind.
Allan: Try to help me understand. The effort would be good for you.
Victim of Boredom: I cannot explain it well because I have no comparison to use. A life on Earth, when it has not been used well, leaves the spirit what fire leaves of paper it has burned... sparks. Those sparks still show what the paper was and what destroyed it.
Victim of Boredom: Those sparks are the memory of earthly ties that run through the spirit until the ashes of the body are fully scattered. Only then does the spirit recover itself as a spiritual being and begin to want to move forward.
Allan: What caused the boredom you complain of?
Victim of Boredom: The results of an earlier life. Boredom is the child of idleness. I did not know how to use the long years I once spent on Earth, and the results of that inactivity still follow me in the spirit world.
Allan: Can spirits like you end that state whenever they want to?
Victim of Boredom: Not always. Their will is paralyzed by the boredom itself. They suffer the results of their former life. They were useless, with no initiative, and they do not help one another.
Victim of Boredom: They are left to themselves until weariness with that empty condition gives them the desire to change it. As soon as that desire starts to wake up, they find help and wise advice that helps them keep going.
Allan: Can you tell me anything about your life on Earth?
Victim of Boredom: Sadly, there is little to tell, as you can understand. Boredom, uselessness, and idleness come from laziness. Laziness is the mother of ignorance.
Allan: Did you make no progress in your former lives?
Victim of Boredom: I advanced a little in all of them, but very little. All our lives reflect one another. A spirit always makes some progress in a life, but sometimes it is so slight that we barely notice it.
Allan: While you wait for a new life, would you like to come to me from time to time?
Victim of Boredom: Call me, so I am forced to come. You would be doing me a service.
Allan: Can you tell me why your handwriting changes so often?
Victim of Boredom: Because you ask me so many questions. It tires me and forces me to get help.
Mediums’ Guide: The effort of thinking is what tires him and makes us help him answer.
Mediums’ Guide: He is one of the lazy ones in the spirit world, just as he was among the living. We brought him to you so you could try to pull him out of an apathy that is truly a state of suffering.
Mediums’ Guide: This suffering can be worse than sharp pain, because it can go on without end. Can you imagine a worse torture than endless weariness?
Mediums’ Guide: Spirits who seek life on Earth only as a distraction, just to break the dull monotony of spirit life, are often like this.
Mediums’ Guide: They return to Earth without a firm decision to become good, and so they must begin again over and over, until they finally feel a sincere desire to advance.
