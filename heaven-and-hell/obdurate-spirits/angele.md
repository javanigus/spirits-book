Bordeaux, 1862. A spirit named Angèle came on her own. She had lived a lazy, empty life and neglected her husband, her children, and her responsibilities.
Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...
Allan: Do you repent of your faults?
Angèle: No.
Allan: Then why do you come to me?
Angèle: To try to do so.
Allan: Are you not happy?
Angèle: No.
Allan: Are you suffering?
Angèle: No.
Allan: What are you missing?
Angèle: Peace.
Some spirits only call it suffering when it feels like physical pain, even when their inner state is unbearable.
Allan: How can you have no peace in the spirit life?
Angèle: Regret for the past.
Allan: Regret for the past is remorse. Then you do repent?
Angèle: No, but I am afraid of the future.
Allan: What are you afraid of?
Angèle: The unknown.
Allan: Will you tell me what you did in your last life? It may help me help you.
Angèle: Nothing.
Allan: What was your position in society?
Angèle: Ordinary.
Allan: Were you married?
Angèle: Yes, and I had children.
Allan: Did you fulfill your duties as a wife and mother?
Angèle: No. My husband tired me, and my children did too.
Allan: How did you spend your time?
Angèle: Enjoying myself when I was young. Then later, being tired of everything.
Allan: What work did you have?
Angèle: None.
Allan: Then who took care of your home?
Angèle: The servant.
Allan: Is that useless life the cause of your regrets and fear now?
Angèle: Perhaps.
Allan: It is not enough to admit that. To make up for your wasted life, will you help the guilty and suffering spirits around you?
Angèle: How?
Allan: By helping them improve, with your advice and your prayers.
Angèle: I do not know how to pray.
Allan: We will pray together. That will teach you. Will you try?
Angèle: No.
Allan: Why not?
Angèle: I am tired.
Mediums’ Guide: We show you different kinds of suffering so people can understand what wrongdoing leads to.
Mediums’ Guide: Angèle was one of those people with no inner drive. Her life was useless to others and to herself. She cared only about pleasure. She could not find the deep joy that comes from embracing family and social duties.
Mediums’ Guide: She spent her youth in shallow amusements. When serious responsibilities came, she found only emptiness around her, because there was only emptiness in her heart.
Mediums’ Guide: She did not commit great crimes, but she also had no real virtues. She made her husband unhappy. Through carelessness and neglect, she damaged her children’s comfort and their future. She harmed their feelings and judgment by her example and by leaving them in the hands of servants she did not even choose carefully.
Mediums’ Guide: A life that produces no good is still guilty, because evil often comes from good not being done.
Mediums’ Guide: Study God’s commandments. Understand that if you do not choose the path of good, you drift toward the other path. A life without active goodness becomes empty.
Allan: May I ask what Angèle’s life was before her last one? Her last life must have come from the one before it.
Mediums’ Guide: She had lived in the dull idleness and uselessness of a convent. Idle and selfish, she wanted to try family life in her last life on Earth, but her spirit made very little progress.
Mediums’ Guide: She kept pushing away the inner voice that warned her of danger. The slope was easy, and she preferred to keep sliding into the abyss instead of making the effort to stop.
Mediums’ Guide: She now sees the danger of this passivity, but she still does not have enough strength to seriously pull herself out of her lazy indifference.
Mediums’ Guide: Pray for her. Stir her. Push her to open her eyes to the light. It is a duty. Do not neglect anything that may help bring her back to the right road.
Mediums’ Guide: Human beings were made for activity. The spirit must act, and the body is a necessary instrument for that work.
Mediums’ Guide: So live out the conditions of your life. Work and develop your intelligence, so your body can serve the spirit well.
Mediums’ Guide: Do not look for rest before its time. The peace people long for comes through work. The more time someone has wasted, the longer the anxious wait for hope.
Mediums’ Guide: So keep working. Do all your duties with zeal and perseverance, and let faith support you in everything.
Mediums’ Guide: The one who faithfully does even the humblest task is far nobler in God’s sight than the person who leaves his own duty for others to do.
Mediums’ Guide: Duties are the steps of the ladder we climb. Miss none of them. Remember that you are always surrounded by friends who help those who trust in God.
