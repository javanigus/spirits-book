Background: Benoist said he had died in 1704. He had been a monk without belief. He entered religious life for status, misused power, corrupted others, and cruelly persecuted those who opposed him. He said prisoners in his monastery were left to starve, and some were killed to silence their cries. He appeared spontaneously to a medium in Bordeaux and said he was suffering horribly.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Medium: What were you in your lifetime?

Benoist: A monk without belief.

Medium: Was your lack of belief your only fault?

Benoist: It was enough to lead me into every other fault.

Medium: Can you give us details of your life? A sincere confession will help you.

Benoist: I had no money, and I was lazy.

Benoist: I entered religious life, not from any calling, but to gain position.

Benoist: I was intelligent, and I made a place for myself.

Benoist: I had influence, and I misused power.

Benoist: I was vicious, and I led into corrupt living those whom I should have led toward virtue.

Benoist: I was hard-hearted, and I persecuted those who dared to blame my excesses.

Benoist: The dungeons of my monastery were full of people I left there to die of hunger.

Benoist: If hunger made them groan too loudly, I had them silenced by a violent death.

Benoist: Since then, I have been paying for my crimes.

Benoist: I suffer all the torments of hell.

Benoist: My victims stir up the fire that devours me without consuming me.

Benoist: I am constantly chased by unsatisfied lust and hunger.

Benoist: My burning lips are tortured by thirst that not one cooling drop relieves.

Benoist: All the elements are unleashed against me.

Benoist: Pray for me.

Medium: Do you not receive some relief from the prayers offered for all the dead?

Benoist: Do you think they have much power?

Benoist: They have over me exactly the power of the prayers I only pretended to offer for others.

Benoist: I did not fulfill my task.

Benoist: So the reward is refused to me.

Medium: Have you repented?

Benoist: Oh, long ago...but only after I had suffered for a long time and horribly.

Benoist: I was deaf to the cries of my innocent victims.

Benoist: Now the Master is deaf to my cries.

Benoist: Justice!

Medium: You admit that God is just. Put your trust in His goodness and call on Him for help.

Benoist: The howling of the demons is louder than my voice.

Benoist: My cries are choked in my throat, for they fill my mouth with boiling pitch.

Benoist: I did it, great...

Background: He could not finish writing the word God.

Medium: Are you still too tied to earthly ideas to understand that the tortures you endure are completely moral?

Benoist: I endure them.

Benoist: I feel them.

Benoist: I see my executioners.

Benoist: Their faces are only too well known to me.

Benoist: Every one of them has a name that pounds in my brain.

Medium: What made you commit such atrocities?

Benoist: The vices that filled me...the brutality of my passions.

Medium: Have you never begged good spirits to help you out of your misery?

Benoist: I see only the demons of hell.

Medium: Were you afraid of them during your life on Earth?

Benoist: No. I feared nothing.

Benoist: Annihilation was what I believed.

Benoist: Pleasure, at any cost, was what I worshiped.

Benoist: The gods of hell have not abandoned me.

Benoist: I gave my life to them.

Benoist: They will never let me go.

Medium: Do you see no end to your suffering?

Benoist: There is no end to what is infinite.

Medium: God's goodness is infinite. There is nothing that cannot end if He wills it.

Benoist: If only He would will it.

Medium: Why did you come to write here?

Benoist: I do not know how it happened.

Benoist: But I wanted to speak, just as I would want to cry out to relieve my suffering.

Medium: Do the demons try to stop you from writing?

Benoist: No, but they are beside me.

Benoist: They hear me.

Benoist: That is why I do not want to stop writing.

Medium: Is this the first time you have written like this?

Benoist: Yes.

Medium: Did you know that spirits could communicate with people in this way?

Benoist: No.

Medium: Then how did you find it out?

Benoist: I do not know.

Medium: What feeling did you have when you came to me?

Benoist: A calming of my terrors.

Medium: How did you realize that you were here?

Benoist: It was as if I had woken up from sleep.

Medium: What did you do in order to communicate with me?

Benoist: I do not understand it.

Benoist: Did you not feel how it was?

Medium: I am not asking about what I felt, but about what you felt. Try to see what you are doing now while I write for you.

Benoist: You are my thought.

Benoist: That is all I see of it.

Medium: So you did not choose to make me write?

Benoist: No.

Benoist: It is I who am writing.

Benoist: I think through you.

Medium: Try to see more clearly what you are doing. Good spirits around you will help you understand.

Benoist: No, good angels do not come into hell.

Medium: You are not alone. Look around you. Try to see.

Benoist: I feel that I am being helped to think through you.

Benoist: Your hand obeys me.

Benoist: I do not touch you, and yet I hold you.

Benoist: I do not understand it.

Medium: Ask your protectors for help. We will pray together.

Benoist: You're going to leave me!

Benoist: Stay with me.

Benoist: They'll seize me again!

Benoist: I beg you... stay! Stay!

Medium: I cannot stay any longer. Come back every day. We'll pray together, and good spirits will help you.

Benoist: Yes...I want to ask pardon.

Benoist: Ask for me.

Benoist: I cannot ask for myself.

Background: After the communication, guidance was given about his condition.

Mediums’ Guide: Courage.

Mediums’ Guide: What you ask for this spirit will be granted, but his atonement is far from over.

Mediums’ Guide: The atrocities he committed are unspeakable and countless.

Mediums’ Guide: His guilt is even greater because he had intelligence, education, and knowledge.

Mediums’ Guide: He sinned with full awareness of the evil in his conduct, and so his sufferings are terrible.

Mediums’ Guide: But with the help and example of prayer, they will be lessened, because he will know they can come to an end, and hope will sustain him.

Mediums’ Guide: God sees that he is on the road to repentance, and that is why he has been allowed to communicate with you, so that he may be encouraged and sustained.

Mediums’ Guide: Think of him often.

Mediums’ Guide: We leave him in your care, so that with your guidance you may strengthen the good resolutions that will form in him.

Mediums’ Guide: His repentance will be followed by the desire to repair his crimes.

Mediums’ Guide: He will then ask for a new life on Earth, to practice goodness instead of the evil he has done.

Mediums’ Guide: And when the Lord sees that he is sincere and firm in his good intentions, he will be helped to see the divine light that will guide him to safety, and he will be welcomed back like the returning prodigal son.

Mediums’ Guide: Be confident of success.

Mediums’ Guide: We will help you in this task.

Background: Allan places Benoist among repentant criminals even though human justice never punished him, because true criminality lies in the evil done, not only in the punishment given by earthly courts.
