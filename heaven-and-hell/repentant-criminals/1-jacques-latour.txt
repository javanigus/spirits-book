Background: Jacques Latour was a notorious murderer, condemned and executed in September 1864. At a private Spiritist meeting in Brussels, a woman medium suddenly began writing without anyone calling him. Her hand moved violently, scratching huge letters onto the paper. No one there had been thinking about him.

Narration: The medium's hand begins writing violently...

Jacques Latour: I repent! I repent! Latour.

Medium: What made you come here instead of somewhere else, since we did not call you?

Jacques Latour: I saw that you were compassionate, and that you would pity me.

Jacques Latour: Others have called me more out of curiosity than charity...or they shrink back from me in horror.

Background: The spirit identified strongly with the medium. Her face, voice, and gestures changed. His despair was so intense and painful that everyone there was deeply moved.

Jacques Latour: Oh yes...pity! You do not know how much I need it, because you do not know how much I suffer!

Jacques Latour: It is suffering with no pause and no rest...and no end!

Jacques Latour: I understand that now.

Jacques Latour: Pity, Lord!

Jacques Latour: I beg You, do not be unyielding.

Jacques Latour: You who hear me...you are kind and charitable.

Jacques Latour: You will pray for me.

Jacques Latour: Oh, I beg you, do it.

Jacques Latour: Do not reject me.

Jacques Latour: Ask God to remove this horrible sight from before my eyes.

Jacques Latour: He will hear you, because you are good.

Jacques Latour: I beg you... do not reject me as I rejected others.

Jacques Latour: Pray for me.

Background: Those present told him that God asks for sincere repentance and the desire to make up for evil. They promised to pray for him and recited the prayer for suffering and repentant spirits.

Jacques Latour: Thank You, my God!

Jacques Latour: Oh, thank You!

Jacques Latour: You have had pity on me.

Jacques Latour: Those horrible images are going away.

Jacques Latour: Do not leave me.

Jacques Latour: Send me good spirits to support me.

Jacques Latour: Thank You!

Background: The next day, at another gathering, the same spirit returned. The scene began again for a few minutes with the same intense display, but less violently.

Narration: The medium's hand begins writing again with feverish agitation...

Jacques Latour: Thank you for your prayers.

Jacques Latour: I am already better because of them.

Jacques Latour: I prayed to God so earnestly that He granted me a lessening of my sufferings...but only for a moment.

Jacques Latour: For I will see them again...my victims.

Jacques Latour: There they are! There they are!

Jacques Latour: Do you see this blood?

Jacques Latour: Forgive me for taking hold of you like this.

Jacques Latour: That is my punishment.

Jacques Latour: I deserved it, my God...but have pity on me!

Jacques Latour: All of you, pray for me!

Jacques Latour: Pity me!

Background: Later, a member of the Paris Spiritist Society prayed for him and received several more communications.

Jacques Latour: I was called almost right after my death, but I could not communicate at once, and many frivolous spirits took my name and my place.

Jacques Latour: I took advantage of the presence in Brussels of the President of the Paris Society, and with permission from higher spirits, I was able to communicate.

Jacques Latour: I will come and speak with the Society.

Jacques Latour: I will begin repairing my faults by giving warnings that may serve as a lesson to all criminals who read about my sufferings.

Jacques Latour: Sermons about hellfire do little to affect hardened criminals.

Jacques Latour: They are less afraid of devils than of policemen.

Jacques Latour: No priest can say to them, "I have seen all this with my own eyes. I have witnessed the tortures of the damned."

Jacques Latour: Ah, then, more than one will draw back from the edge of the abyss he was about to fall into.

Jacques Latour: And every miserable person I stop on the road to crime will be part of the atonement for the evil I once did.
