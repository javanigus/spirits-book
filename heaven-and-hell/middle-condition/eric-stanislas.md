In August 1863, during a meeting of the Spiritist Society of Paris, a spirit named Eric Stanislas spontaneously communicated through a medium. He explained that he had previously been an unhappy, wandering spirit, but had recently begun improving through contact with Spiritist gatherings and the influence of sincere, charitable people.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Eric Stanislas: How much happiness we spirits feel when we witness the sincere emotions flowing between kind and loving hearts.

Eric Stanislas: Beautiful principles that open a path of salvation for everything that lives and breathes, both physically and spiritually...

Eric Stanislas: May your beneficial influence spread widely across the Earth and throughout the spirit world.

Eric Stanislas: Dear friends, what words could possibly express the joy felt by your brothers and sisters beyond the grave when we see the selfless affection uniting all of you?

Eric Stanislas: Ah, brothers... your doctrine will spread immense good and deep conviction among humanity.

Eric Stanislas: And what a harvest of blessings you yourselves will one day receive because of the good you are accomplishing.

Eric Stanislas: I have remained among you throughout this entire evening.

Eric Stanislas: I listened.

Eric Stanislas: I understood.

Eric Stanislas: And now I too will be able to fulfill my duty by instructing imperfect spirits in the next life.

Eric Stanislas: Listen carefully.

Eric Stanislas: I was once far from happy.

Eric Stanislas: Lost within the immensity of infinity, my suffering became even more painful because I could not clearly understand its cause.

Eric Stanislas: Thanks be to God...

Eric Stanislas: In His goodness, He allowed me to enter this sanctuary, a place where wicked spirits cannot come without consequences.

Eric Stanislas: How grateful I am to you, my friends.

Eric Stanislas: How much strength I have gained from being among you.

Eric Stanislas: Keep meeting together often...

Eric Stanislas: You who are united by hope and charity.

Eric Stanislas: You cannot imagine how much good is produced through your sincere and serious gatherings.

Eric Stanislas: Spirits who still have much to learn...

Eric Stanislas: Spirits who remained voluntarily idle, inactive, and forgetful of their duties...

Eric Stanislas: Such spirits may sometimes be brought among you, whether by chance or through spiritual guidance.

Eric Stanislas: Suddenly struck by a powerful inner shock, they may begin reflecting upon themselves.

Eric Stanislas: And this happens often.

Eric Stanislas: They begin seeing their true condition.

Eric Stanislas: They begin understanding the goal they must reach.

Eric Stanislas: And strengthened by the example you provide, many seek the means to free themselves from their painful condition.

Eric Stanislas: I am very happy to speak on behalf of those suffering spirits.

Eric Stanislas: Because I am speaking to people with compassionate hearts.

Eric Stanislas: And I know I will not be rejected by you.

Eric Stanislas: Once again, generous friends...

Eric Stanislas: Allow me to express my personal gratitude, along with the gratitude of all your friends in the spirit world.

Eric Stanislas: You have done immense good for us...

Eric Stanislas: Perhaps without even realizing how much.

After Eric Stanislas finished speaking, the Mediums’ Guide added an explanation concerning his spiritual condition.

Mediums’ Guide: My children, the spirit who has just communicated with you was unhappy for a very long time because he remained on a wrong path.

Mediums’ Guide: But he has now recognized his mistakes.

Mediums’ Guide: He sincerely repented of his wrongdoing.

Mediums’ Guide: And at last he turned back toward God, from whom he had previously turned away.

Mediums’ Guide: His condition is not yet completely happy...

Mediums’ Guide: But he now aspires toward happiness.

Mediums’ Guide: And because of that, he no longer suffers as before.

Mediums’ Guide: He is now permitted to come listen to the teachings given to you by your spirit-friends.

Mediums’ Guide: Soon he will also be allowed to enter a lower spiritual level where he will instruct and assist other spirits who, like himself, once violated God's laws.

Mediums’ Guide: This service is the reparation now required of him.

Mediums’ Guide: Through this work, he will gradually earn happiness...

Mediums’ Guide: Because he now truly desires to become better.
