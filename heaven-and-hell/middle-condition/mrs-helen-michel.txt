Background: Mrs. Hélèn Michel died suddenly at the age of twenty-five from a cause doctors couldn't determine. She was wealthy, kind-hearted, gentle, charitable, and generous, but also somewhat frivolous and overly focused on the pleasures and distractions of life on Earth. Three days after her death, she was evoked by people who had known her personally.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I do not know where I am...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: What confusion surrounds me...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: You called me, and so I came...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: But I cannot understand why I am no longer in my home.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: They are weeping because they believe I am gone...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Yet I am standing beside them.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Why can none of them see me?

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: My body no longer belongs to me...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: And yet I still feel it there, cold and lifeless.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I try to move away from it...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: But something pulls me back toward it again and again.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I feel like two different persons at once.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Oh... when will I finally understand what has happened to me?

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I must go back there...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: What would happen to that other self of mine if I stayed away?

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Farewell...

Background: Allan Kardec explains that this communication clearly shows the feeling of duality often experienced before complete separation from the physical body has fully happened.

Background: Because Mrs. Hélèn Michel had lived a comfortable and somewhat superficial life, strongly attached to earthly pleasures, the separation from the body happened slowly.

Background: Yet because she was fundamentally kind and free from serious vice, her condition was not deeply painful and improved quickly.

Background: A few days later, she was evoked again, and her understanding had already become much clearer.

Narration: The medium’s hand begins writing...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Thank you for praying for me.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I now recognize God's goodness in sparing me fear and suffering during the separation of my soul from my body.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: My poor mother will struggle greatly to accept my death.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: But she will be sustained.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: What now looks to her like a terrible tragedy was necessary for her spiritual growth.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Through this sorrow, she will come to understand that the realities of the next life are the only things of true value.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I will remain beside her until the end of her trial on Earth.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: And I will help her endure it.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I am not unhappy.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: But I still have much work to do before I can rise toward the place of the blessed spirits.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I will pray for permission to return to Earth someday.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I must make up for the time I wasted during my last life on Earth.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Let faith sustain you, my friends.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Trust in the power of sincere prayer when it truly comes from the heart.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: God is good.

Allan: Did it take long before you fully understood your condition?

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I realized I was dead on the day you prayed for me.

Allan: Was your confusion painful?

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: No.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I did not suffer.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I simply thought I was dreaming and expected to wake up.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: My life on Earth was not free from suffering.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Everyone born on Earth has to suffer in some way.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I accepted the will of God during my life on Earth...

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: And God counted that acceptance in my favor.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I am grateful to you for your prayers.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: They helped me regain awareness and understanding.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Thank you.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: I will always come gladly whenever you call me.

Mrs. Hélèn Michel: Farewell.
