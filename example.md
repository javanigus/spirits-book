## The Hierarchy of Spirits

Spirits exist at different levels of development.

These differences are based on what they have learned, the qualities they have developed, and the imperfections they have overcome. This progression is gradual, with no sharp boundaries between levels.

For this reason, any classification is only a way of helping us understand. The divisions are not absolute. Spirits evolve continuously, and their characteristics may overlap.

Even so, their development can be grouped into three main orders.

### The Third Order: Imperfect Spirits

At the lower end are spirits who are still strongly influenced by material concerns.

They are often marked by ignorance, selfishness, pride, and other negative tendencies. Their understanding is limited, and their actions may be careless, misguided, or harmful.

Not all of them are the same.

Some are openly harmful and take pleasure in doing wrong.  
Others are indifferent, doing neither good nor evil.  
Some are simply frivolous or mischievous, causing minor disturbances rather than serious harm.

Their knowledge is often incomplete and confused. Even when they express truths, these are usually mixed with error.

Their condition brings suffering.

They are aware of the happiness of more advanced spirits, and this awareness can lead to feelings such as envy and frustration. They also remain affected by the consequences of their past actions.

Within this group, several types can be recognized:

* Impure spirits – actively inclined toward harm and deception  
* Frivolous spirits – playful, mocking, and often misleading  
* Pseudo-learned spirits – knowledgeable in appearance but mistaken in many ideas  
* Neutral spirits – neither clearly good nor clearly harmful  
* Disturbing spirits – those who produce physical effects or disruptions

### The Second Order: Good Spirits

Above them are spirits who have made significant progress.

They are guided by a sincere desire to do good, and they act in accordance with that intention. While not yet perfect, they are no longer dominated by negative impulses.

Their qualities vary depending on their level of development.

Some are especially knowledgeable  
Others are more developed in wisdom and moral judgment  
The most advanced combine knowledge with goodness

They understand the nature of things more clearly and already experience a form of inner peace.

These spirits often guide and assist others.

They encourage good actions, help people resist harmful influences, and watch over those who are receptive to their influence. Their presence supports growth and moral development.

They may still retain traces of their past, but these no longer dominate their behavior.

Within this order, we can distinguish:

* Benevolent spirits – focused on kindness and protection  
* Learned spirits – distinguished by knowledge  
* Wise spirits – guided by strong moral judgment  
* Higher spirits – combining knowledge, wisdom, and goodness

### The First Order: Pure Spirits

At the highest level are spirits who have reached complete development.

They are no longer influenced by matter and have overcome all imperfections. Their understanding is clear, and their nature is fully aligned with what is good.

They no longer need to undergo trials or reincarnation.

Their state is one of lasting harmony and fulfillment—not inactivity, but purposeful action. They carry out roles that maintain order in the universe.

They guide other spirits, assist in their development, and help direct the unfolding of creation.

These are the beings often referred to as angels.

They do not exist to be commanded, but to serve a higher order. While communication with them is possible, they do not act according to human will.

### Progress and Continuity

Spirits do not remain fixed in any of these orders.

All are created with the ability to progress, and all move forward over time.

This progression is not always even.

A spirit may advance more in knowledge than in morality, or vice versa, which is why characteristics can sometimes appear mixed.

For this reason, classification is only a guide.

What matters is not the category itself, but the direction of growth.

All spirits are moving—at different speeds—toward greater understanding, purity, and alignment with the good.
