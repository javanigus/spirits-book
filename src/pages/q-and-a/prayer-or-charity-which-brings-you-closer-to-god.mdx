# Prayer or Charity: Which Brings You Closer to God?

*According to The Spirits' Book*

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-01.webp" alt="Title card: Prayer or Charity: Which Brings You Closer to God?" maxWidth={1100} />

Some people say prayer is the way to grow closer to God. Others say it is helping people in need. So which matters more: prayer, charity, or something else? The Spirits' Book gives an answer that may surprise you.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-02.webp" alt="Where these answers come from. The Spirits' Book: published in 1857. Allan Kardec: the French teacher who asked the questions. Advanced spirits: wise, good souls who grew through many lives. Mediums: people through whom spirits communicate." maxWidth={1100} />

First, a quick note on where these answers come from. The Spirits' Book was published in 1857 by Allan Kardec, a French teacher. Kardec asked more than a thousand questions about God, life and death. The answers came from highly advanced spirits. These are souls of people who once lived on earth, and who became wise and good through many lives and many trials. Some people call them angels. They answered through people called mediums. Not all spirits are good, so when we say "the spirits," we mean these advanced spirits.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-03.webp" alt="Statement card: Charity comes first, but prayer and knowing yourself help." maxWidth={1100} />

The spirits' answer is that we need more than one. Charity is the highest virtue, and the truest sign of spiritual growth. But charity works together with prayer, with knowing ourselves, and with overcoming selfishness. Each one supports the others.

## Charity: the highest virtue

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-04.webp" alt="Charity: the highest virtue. Charity is the source of all virtues. Illustration: a tree full of small lights, growing from a glowing heart at its roots." maxWidth={1100} />

According to the spirits, the highest virtue, and the truest measure of spiritual growth, is selfless charity: helping others without expecting anything in return. Just as selfishness is the root of all faults, charity is the source of all virtues.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-05.webp" alt="Charity: the highest virtue. Charity is more than giving money. Illustration: three numbered circles labeled Kindness, Patience and Forgiveness." maxWidth={1100} />

And charity is much more than giving money. The spirits define it as kindness toward everyone, patience with other people's faults, and forgiveness when we are wronged.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-06.webp" alt="Charity: the highest virtue. Good deeds are the best prayers. Illustration: one person helping another, with a glowing heart between them." maxWidth={1100} />

The spirits also said that good deeds are the best prayers, because actions are worth more than words. A person who only prays or studies, but never practices charity, does not grow spiritually.

## The key to improving yourself

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-07.webp" alt="The key to improving yourself. The best way to improve: know yourself. Illustration: a person looking into a tall mirror at a glowing reflection of themselves." maxWidth={1100} />

When Allan Kardec asked the spirits for the most effective way to improve ourselves in this life and resist evil, they answered with an ancient saying: know yourself.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-08.webp" alt="The key to improving yourself. Review your day every evening. Illustration: a crescent moon above a checklist, with a person standing beside it." maxWidth={1100} />

One of the advanced spirits explained how to do this. At the end of every day, examine your conscience. Review what you did, and why you did it. Ask yourself: Did I neglect any duty? Did I harm anyone? Did I act out of pride or selfishness?

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-09.webp" alt="The key to improving yourself. Pull out faults like weeds. Illustration: a weed being pulled out of the ground between two golden flowers." maxWidth={1100} />

Knowing yourself helps you find your hidden faults, overcome vanity, and pull out selfish habits, like pulling weeds from a garden.

## What prayer really does

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-10.webp" alt="What prayer really does. Prayer brings help from good spirits. Illustration: a person with small lights rising toward one bright light above." maxWidth={1100} />

Prayer is not a replacement for effort, but it is an important support. Sincere prayer lifts the soul and connects us with God. It also draws protecting spirits and guardian angels, who inspire good thoughts and strengthen our will against temptation.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-11.webp" alt="What prayer really does. Prayer gives strength and patience. Illustration: a person standing calmly inside a circle of light while rain falls from dark clouds." maxWidth={1100} />

Prayer does not cancel the trials we need to go through. But it gives us the moral strength, patience and clarity to face difficulties with courage.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-12.webp" alt="What prayer really does. Prayer without effort is useless. Illustration: a balance scale with only asking on the lighter side and people taking action on the heavier side." maxWidth={1100} />

But prayer without real effort to improve is useless. The spirits said that God helps those who act, not those who only ask.

## Growing beyond selfishness

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-13.webp" alt="Growing beyond selfishness. Pride and selfishness are the biggest barriers. Illustration: a person facing a wall of blocks labeled Pride and Selfishness, with a bright light behind it." maxWidth={1100} />

Beyond these practices, the spirits describe spiritual growth as a complete change inside us. The biggest barriers between people and God are pride and selfishness. We grow as we free ourselves from material desires and animal instincts, and learn to put the well-being of others before our own.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-14.webp" alt="Growing beyond selfishness. Grow in knowledge and in goodness. Illustration: two ladders: a shorter one labeled Knowledge and a taller, glowing one labeled Goodness." maxWidth={1100} />

Spirits grow in two ways: in knowledge, by gaining understanding and reason, and in goodness, by overcoming faults and practicing kindness. Intelligence helps us understand God's laws. But only moral growth, becoming a better person, raises a spirit to a higher level.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god/prayer-or-charity-which-brings-you-closer-to-god-15.webp" alt="A person with small lights rising toward one bright light above, under the words: Know yourself. Pray for strength. Practice charity." maxWidth={1100} />

So, prayer or charity? According to the spirits, the best path to spiritual growth uses all of them. Knowing yourself shows you what you need to fix. Prayer gives you the strength and guidance to resist evil. And selfless charity puts God's law into practice every day, by overcoming selfishness and serving others. These answers come from The Spirits' Book. You can read a simple version of it at lifedeathbeyondbook.com.
