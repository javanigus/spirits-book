# Is There One True Religion?

*According to The Spirits' Book*

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-01.webp" alt="Title card: Is There One True Religion?" maxWidth={1100} />

Christians, Muslims, Jews, Hindus, Buddhists and many others each believe their faith is the right one. So is there one true religion? And if there is, which one is it? The Spirits' Book gives an answer that may surprise you.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-02.webp" alt="Where these answers come from. The Spirits' Book: published in 1857. Allan Kardec: the French teacher who asked the questions. Advanced spirits: wise, good souls who grew through many lives. Mediums: people through whom spirits communicate." maxWidth={1100} />

First, a quick note on where these answers come from. The Spirits' Book was published in 1857 by Allan Kardec, a French teacher. Kardec asked more than a thousand questions about God, life and death. The answers came from highly advanced spirits. These are souls of people who once lived on earth, and who became wise and good through many lives and many trials. Some people call them angels. They answered through people called mediums. Not all spirits are good, so when we say 'the spirits,' we mean these advanced spirits.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-03.webp" alt="Many glowing paths climbing a dark mountain from different sides, all meeting at one bright light at the top." maxWidth={1100} />

The spirits' answer is clear: no single religion owns the truth or holds the only key to salvation. God does not judge us by the name of our religion. God judges us only by how we behave, and by the good we do.

## The true test of any religion

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-04.webp" alt="The true test of any religion. The best religion has the fewest hypocrites. Illustration: a balance scale with a religion's claims on the lighter side and moral people on the heavier side." maxWidth={1100} />

Every religion says it alone has the truth. So how can we tell which one is right? The spirits gave a simple test. The true religion is the one that produces the fewest hypocrites and the most moral people, people who practice the law of love and charity in its purest form, toward everyone.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-05.webp" alt="The true test of any religion. A religion that divides people is wrong. Illustration: two groups of people separated by a crack." maxWidth={1100} />

A religion that divides people and sets them against each other can only be wrong and harmful.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-06.webp" alt="The true test of any religion. Judge a religion by how it changes people. Illustration: a dim figure, an arrow, and the same figure glowing brightly." maxWidth={1100} />

So a religion should not be judged by its official beliefs, its claim to be the only true faith, or how old its traditions are. It should be judged by how much it changes its followers for the better.

## One God, worshipped by all peoples

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-07.webp" alt="One God, worshipped by all peoples. All peoples worship the same God. Illustration: the word God in many languages arranged around one light labeled One God." maxWidth={1100} />

The spirits also said that all peoples worship the same God, whatever name they use. Goodness is always goodness, whether it is done in the name of Allah, Yahweh or the Heavenly Father, because there is only one God over the whole universe.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-08.webp" alt="One God, worshipped by all peoples. God has no favorite language or ceremony. Illustration: prayers in different languages all reaching one glowing heart." maxWidth={1100} />

Does God prefer one form of worship? The spirits said that is like asking whether God prefers to be worshipped in one language rather than another. Prayers reach God through the heart, not through rituals.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-09.webp" alt="One God, worshipped by all peoples. Hatred in God's name goes against God's law. Illustration: crossed swords inside a no symbol." maxWidth={1100} />

This is why the spirits spoke out against holy wars, attacking people for their faith, and hatred between religious groups. They said these come from evil spirits, because God wants all people to love their neighbors as themselves. Fighting people, or cursing them, because their beliefs are different goes directly against God's law.

## Truth is found in all religions

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-10.webp" alt="Truth is found in all religions. Truth is revealed gradually, like light. Illustration: the sun rising over the horizon." maxWidth={1100} />

Then why do religions teach different things? The spirits explained that truth is like light: humanity has to get used to it gradually, over many centuries.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-11.webp" alt="Truth is found in all religions. Every religion holds seeds of great truths. Illustration: several small plants growing from the ground in warm light." maxWidth={1100} />

That is why we should not reject older faiths and traditions. All religions contain the seeds of great truths, even though those truths are often mixed with incomplete stories, symbols, or superstitions from their time and culture.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-12.webp" alt="Truth is found in all religions. Do to others what you would want them to do to you. Illustration: two people facing each other with a glowing heart between them." maxWidth={1100} />

And through all true teachings runs the same summary of God's law, a rule found in nearly every religion: "Do to others what you would want them to do to you." Any faith that helps a person overcome pride and selfishness, and practice charity without expecting anything in return, is following God's law.

## Not even Spiritism is required

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-13.webp" alt="Not even Spiritism is required. Happiness after death does not depend on Spiritism. Illustration: people walking toward an open, glowing door." maxWidth={1100} />

The spirits even applied this to their own teachings. Allan Kardec asked whether a person must accept these spirit teachings, known as Spiritism, to be happy in the afterlife. The spirits said no. If it were true, everyone who did not believe, or never had the chance to learn about it, would be shut out from happiness, and that makes no sense.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-14.webp" alt="Not even Spiritism is required. The good is always the good, whatever path leads to it. Illustration: a glowing heart with rays of light." maxWidth={1100} />

Instead, they said, only the good brings future happiness. The good is always the good, whatever path leads to it.

<SectionImage src="/img/q-and-a/is-there-one-true-religion/is-there-one-true-religion-15.webp" alt="The glowing mountain paths again, all meeting at one light, with the words: The true religion: love, justice and charity toward all." maxWidth={1100} />

So, is there one true religion? According to the spirits, the true religion is not an organization or a building. It is the practice of love, justice and charity toward all beings, wherever it is found. These answers come from The Spirits' Book. You can read a simple version of it at lifedeathbeyondbook.com.
